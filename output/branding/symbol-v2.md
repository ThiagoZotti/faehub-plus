## Prompt utilizado

Use case: logo-brand. Design a completely new standalone symbol for FaeHub+, a Brazilian academic app. ONLY THE SYMBOL. Absolutely no wordmark, letters as text, captions, slogans, extra plus sign, presentation sheet or mockup. Genuine transparent PNG alpha background. Art direction: an original architectural 'open campus' glyph, not a stock SaaS logo. Form a compact asymmetric monolith from one continuous broad cobalt-blue shape. A subtle F should emerge from two carefully spaced open negative-space cuts, while the vertical void also suggests an open doorway. The upper horizontal stroke has one distinctive oblique incision at its inner junction, echoed only once in the lower cut. Squared, precisely balanced silhouette with lightly softened outer corners and crisp structural interior cuts. Make the whole symbol visually resolved and memorable as a single glyph, not an assembly of icons. Deliberate proportions and negative space, Swiss identity-design discipline, contemporary editorial-tech sensibility. One flat ink color #2563EB throughout; no gradient whatsoever, no shading, no texture, no glow, no shadows, no 3D, no outline, no badge container, no enclosing circle. Avoid a generic rounded F typed in a font; avoid arrows, swooshes, infinity loops, hexagons, nodes, books, graduation caps. Center one finished compact mark on a square transparent canvas with balanced modest clear space. It must remain recognizable in a single-color 24px app header and favicon. Minimalism through a very specific silhouette, not decoration. No checkerboard graphic: actual transparency.

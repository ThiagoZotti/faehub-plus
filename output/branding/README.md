Use case: logo-brand. Create one finished original logo for the Brazilian academic app FaeHub+. Asset type: clean professional horizontal brand lockup, transparent PNG with real alpha channel, not a mockup. Design a distinctive minimal geometric monogram that combines a clear F with the idea of a connected campus, with a small integrated plus sign. The mark should be structurally simple: two or three solid shapes with softly rounded corners and precise negative space; strong readable silhouette at 24 pixels. Place the symbol to the left of a refined custom sans-serif wordmark reading exactly "FaeHub+" (capital F, capital H, lowercase ae and ub, plus sign). Balanced contemporary rounded geometric lettering, slightly tailored to the monogram, carefully kerned. Deep navy #102746 wordmark, crisp cobalt blue #306CF5 symbol and plus sign, optional very restrained lighter blue in a small part of the symbol only. Flat solid colors, clean crisp vector-like edges, mature yet approachable school/technology identity. Generous but economical transparent padding; horizontal centered composition. No gradients, no 3D, no shadows, no glow, no glass, no outer emblem circle, no academic cap, no book illustration, no extra text or tagline, no presentation board, no color palette swatches, no opaque background, no drawn checkerboard. Output only the logo on actual transparent background.
